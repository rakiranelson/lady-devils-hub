## Project Overview
The purpose of this software is to be a centralized hub for the Duke Women’s Flag Football club that can reduce the need for Exec to use several platforms, such as When2Meet, Google Sheets, and GroupMe, to manage event scheduling, tournament logistics, and travel logistics, including transportation, lodging, and communicating these details to players. The intended users are Exec members and players on the team. As a new club team at Duke, it would be ideal for much of the day-to-day team operations to be simplified, less time-consuming, and easier to manage so that Exec can focus on club growth, player retention, team improvement, and planning events such as outreach and community activities. Because members of Exec are also students, making the administrative side of running the team easier to manage is one of the primary goals. The software will also provide players with a consistent place to find and revisit important information about practices, tournaments, and other team activities, without needing to search through GroupMe history.
